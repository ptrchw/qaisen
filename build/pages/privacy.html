Privacy Policy

How QAISEN collects, uses and protects your information, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

QAISEN Pte. Ltd. · Effective [Publication date] · Last updated [Revision date]

1. Our commitment to privacy

At QAISEN, we believe businesses should be able to explore opportunities for improvement without unnecessarily exposing personal or confidential information.

This Privacy Policy explains how QAISEN Pte. Ltd. (“QAISEN”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, processes, protects and retains information when you visit qaisen.com, contact us or use our free AI Reality Check.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for the services requested and to handle personal data in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (“PDPA”).

This policy also explains the limitations of confidentiality when users voluntarily submit business information to an online diagnostic.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to the QAISEN website.
  • Users of AI Reality Check.
  • Individuals who submit enquiries or contact forms.
  • Individuals who request consultations or information about QAISEN services.
  • Other individuals whose personal data may be processed through our website.

Separate privacy or confidentiality arrangements may apply to paid consulting engagements and will be addressed in the relevant engagement documentation.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you voluntarily provide

Depending on how you interact with QAISEN, we may collect:

  • Your name, business email address and contact details when you choose to contact us.
  • Your organisation name, role or business enquiry where voluntarily provided.
  • Information submitted through consultation or enquiry forms.
  • Business challenges, workflow descriptions, objectives and constraints entered into AI Reality Check.
  • Other information you voluntarily include in communications with us.

AI Reality Check does not require users to provide their name, email address or account details to generate a preliminary report.

3.2 Technical information

When you visit our website, technical information may be processed by our website infrastructure and service providers.

Depending on the services enabled, this may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Requested pages and technical events.
  • Security and error logs.
  • Cookie or analytics information, where applicable.

Some technical information may constitute personal data under applicable law.

3.3 Information we ask you not to provide

Please do not submit personal identification numbers, passwords, financial account credentials, patient records, customer or employee personal data, confidential contracts, trade secrets or other sensitive information through AI Reality Check.

The diagnostic is designed to work with general business descriptions rather than identifiable records or confidential documents.

If personal or confidential information is entered voluntarily, it may be processed as part of the assessment before it can be identified or removed.

4. How we use information

We may process information for the following purposes:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Generating the AI Reality Check assessment and report requested by the user.
  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Communicating about services where requested or otherwise permitted by law.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving service reliability.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not require users to submit contact details as a condition of viewing an AI Reality Report.

We do not use AI Reality Check submissions to build a marketing contact list.

Any optional marketing communications will be managed separately and in accordance with applicable consent and communication requirements.

5. How AI Reality Check processes information

AI Reality Check is a preliminary business diagnostic.

When you complete the assessment:

  1. You provide information about a business challenge.
  2. The system evaluates your answers using structured assessment rules and, where enabled, artificial intelligence services.
  3. It generates preliminary findings, supporting explanations and suggested next steps.
  4. You may review the report and save a PDF copy for your own records.

The assessment is intended to support business exploration. It does not independently verify the information supplied.

Where an external AI processing provider is used, relevant submitted content may be transmitted to that provider for the purpose of generating the requested assessment.

The identity of the AI provider, processing location, retention arrangements and applicable contractual safeguards will be confirmed before the service is launched.

QAISEN intends to select and configure AI services so that submitted diagnostic information is not used to train general-purpose AI models without an appropriate legal basis and the necessary authorisation.

6. Temporary sessions and report retention

AI Reality Check is designed to operate without user accounts or a permanent report archive.

Under the intended configuration:

  • Assessment answers are held temporarily while the user completes the diagnostic.
  • Findings and report content remain available during the active session.
  • QAISEN does not maintain a permanent application database of completed AI Reality Reports.
  • The website does not provide a facility for retrieving previous reports.
  • Users can clear their current assessment and start again.
  • Closing or refreshing the page may result in the loss of unsaved assessment information.

Please save your AI Reality Report as a PDF before leaving the page or clearing your assessment. QAISEN does not maintain a saved report copy for later retrieval.

Clearing an assessment removes the active information from the website application's working state.

It does not delete PDF files already saved to your device or necessarily remove technical records previously created by infrastructure or third-party service providers.

Such records are governed by the applicable security, operational and provider retention arrangements described in this policy.

7. Cookies, analytics and browser storage

QAISEN aims to minimise unnecessary tracking.

The website may use essential technical mechanisms required for security, functionality and reliable operation.

If analytics, non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe:

  • The technologies used.
  • Their purposes.
  • The categories of information collected.
  • Relevant service providers.
  • Available consent or preference controls, where required.

AI Reality Check is intended not to use persistent browser storage to save assessment answers or completed reports.

Actual cookie and storage behaviour will be verified before public launch.

8. Disclosure to service providers

QAISEN may engage authorised third-party providers to support website hosting, security, communications, analytics or AI processing.

Where necessary for the stated purposes, information may be disclosed to such providers.

We aim to select providers that offer appropriate confidentiality, security and data-handling safeguards.

Service providers should process information only for authorised purposes and in accordance with applicable contractual obligations.

QAISEN does not sell personal data to advertisers.

We may disclose information where required or authorised by applicable law, or where reasonably necessary to protect lawful rights, security or operations.

9. Overseas processing and transfers

Some website infrastructure or AI service providers may operate outside Singapore.

Where personal data is transferred outside Singapore, QAISEN will take steps required under the PDPA to ensure that applicable transfer requirements are met, including appropriate safeguards where necessary.

The countries involved and the relevant provider arrangements will be assessed before deployment.

10. Data security and confidentiality

QAISEN aims to apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

These may include:

  • Encrypted connections.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Appropriate access controls.
  • Secure configuration of hosting and AI services.
  • Minimisation of unnecessary logging.
  • Security monitoring and maintenance.
  • Appropriate retention and deletion procedures.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should therefore avoid entering sensitive personal information or commercially confidential material into the free diagnostic.

Use of AI Reality Check does not, by itself, establish a professional confidentiality agreement or non-disclosure agreement between the user and QAISEN.

Where a business requires a formal confidentiality commitment, that should be addressed through a separate written agreement before sensitive information is shared.

11. Retention of personal data

QAISEN will retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or where retention remains necessary for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Different information may have different retention periods.

For example:

  • Active AI Reality Check answers are intended to be temporary and not permanently archived by the application.
  • Contact enquiries may be retained for the time reasonably necessary to respond and manage related business communications.
  • Security and technical logs may be retained for limited operational or security purposes.
  • Records associated with a paid engagement may be governed by separate contractual, accounting or legal requirements.

Actual retention periods for contact records, technical logs and third-party processors will be established and documented before publication.

Where retention is no longer necessary, QAISEN will take appropriate steps to delete, anonymise or otherwise cease retaining personal data in accordance with applicable requirements.

12. Your rights and choices

Subject to the PDPA and applicable exceptions, individuals may request access to or correction of personal data held by QAISEN.

You may also withdraw consent for particular uses or disclosures of personal data by giving reasonable notice, subject to applicable legal requirements and any consequences of withdrawal.

You may contact our designated Data Protection Officer to:

  • Ask how your personal data is handled.
  • Request access to or correction of relevant personal data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Raise a privacy concern or complaint.

Please note that QAISEN may not be able to retrieve an AI Reality Check assessment after the temporary session has ended, because the application is not intended to maintain an identifiable report archive.

We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

13. Data breaches and security incidents

QAISEN will assess suspected personal data breaches and take appropriate containment, investigation and remediation measures.

Where a breach is notifiable under the PDPA, QAISEN will make the required notifications to the relevant authorities and affected individuals within applicable legal requirements.

14. Children's personal data

The website and AI Reality Check are intended for business users and are not designed for children.

We do not intentionally request children's personal data through the diagnostic.

If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

15. External websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites.

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

QAISEN is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of websites that we do not operate.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, providers or legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with its effective or revision date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for materially different processing purposes.

How I judge the fit

Three lenses.
One honest answer.

An AI idea is only the right fix if it passes all three: people will use it, your team can deliver it with the data you have, and it pays back. It's a proven design-led innovation model, and every engagement checks all three, so you invest only where AI earns its place.

User research, workflow review and usability testing: Evidence from your staff and customers shows whether a change will be used, or worked around.

Through AI Opportunity Assessment and UX Audit

Data readiness, tool options and rapid prototyping: A realistic view of what AI can do with your data, systems and skills today.

Through Workshops and Discovery Sprint

Impact and effort prioritisation against your goals: A clear go, pivot or stop before you commit budget/scale.

Through AI Opportunity Assessment and Discovery Sprint

Where all three meet: the Discovery Sprint tests desirability, feasibility and viability together with a prototype, before you build.

My approach

Every AI decision starts in a mess. I help you discover what matters.

Many AI options, little guidance, vendor promises and competing priorities. I start from your business problem, not the tool, sift the evidence with you and give you a clear answer: AI fits, partly fits or doesn't. “AI isn't the right fix” is a good answer if it's the true one.

  1. 01 · Frame

    Start from the problem

    Define the business problem and what success looks like, before anyone picks a tool.

  2. 02 · Test

    Check it against evidence

    On your own with the free AI Reality Check, in an expert assessment, or in a facilitated sprint.

  3. 03 · Find

    Get a clear answer

    AI fits, partly fits or doesn't fit, with the reasons and the evidence behind it.

  4. 04 · Decide

    Choose the next step

    A prototype, a build, a simpler fix or a stop, decided together and scoped from the findings.

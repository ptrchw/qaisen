Is this for you?

The assessment suits established SMEs where the owner or MD makes the call, without an in-house AI or digital team, and wants a straight answer before committing budget.

  • You keep hearing you need AI, but can't see where it would actually help.
  • Vendors are pitching tools, and you can't compare them against your real problem.
  • Costs are rising and you want to grow without adding headcount at the same rate.
  • An earlier AI or digital project stalled, and you don't want to repeat it.
  • You need a clear, evidence-backed case to take to your board or partners.

What happens, step by step

Frame, test, find, decide. AI speeds up my analysis; I review every finding and make the call.

  1. Frame the problem

    Two one-hour sessions on your business goals, the problem you want to solve and the key workflows to review, before anyone talks about tools.

    Step 1

  2. Evidence scan

    I go through what you already have: staff feedback, customer complaints and reviews, current tools and the data behind each workflow.

    Step 2

  3. Workflow review

    Up to three key workflows mapped step by step with the people who run them, and checked through three lenses: will people use it, can your team deliver it, and will it pay back?

    Step 3

  4. The finding

    Each workflow is rated: AI fits, partly fits or doesn't fit, with the reasons, the risks and a simpler fix where one exists.

    Step 4

  5. Readout and decision

    A facilitated session with your leadership to walk through the findings and agree the next step together.

    Step 5

Questions

What if AI isn't the answer?

Then that's the finding, and it's a good one. Knowing that a simpler process change, a better form or an existing tool will do saves you far more than it costs.

How is this different from the UX Audit?

The UX Audit looks at a live product and finds why users get stuck. The assessment looks at how your business works and finds where AI could help. Both use the same evidence-based method.

Do we need clean data or an AI strategy first?

No. Part of the assessment is a realistic look at the data and systems you have today, and what they would need for AI to work.

How do you handle our company data?

I'm happy to work under your NDA. Before we start, we agree how your data is handled, including which AI tools, if any, may be used with it.

Can you help after the assessment?

Yes, if you want it. A Discovery Sprint can test the strongest idea with a prototype and real users, and Applied AI Workshops can train your team. Any follow-on work is scoped separately, based on the findings.

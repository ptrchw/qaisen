Is this for you?

The audit suits teams with a product already in users' hands, or about to be.

  • Users drop off at sign-up, checkout or onboarding, and you don't know why.
  • Support keeps answering the same “how do I…?” questions.
  • Your team disagrees about what to fix first.
  • You're about to launch and want a senior eye on it before customers see it.
  • You have no UX designer in-house, or yours is fully booked.

What happens, step by step

No analytics set-up needed. The evidence comes from what your customers are already telling you.

  1. Requirements

    Two one-hour sessions on your goals, your users, the three journeys to review, and access to the product.

    Week 1

  2. Feedback scan

    I go through your support emails, app-store or Google reviews and sales complaints to see where users already struggle.

    Week 1

  3. Heuristic and flow review

    Three key journeys on one platform, checked step by step against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics. Every issue gets a severity rating.

    Weeks 1–2

  4. Synthesis

    Findings, a severity-rated issue log and a draft fix list, ready for your team to weigh up.

    Week 2

  5. Prioritisation workshop

    A facilitated session of two to three hours where your stakeholders score each fix for impact and effort together. Only your team knows the effort, so they decide.

    Weeks 2–3

  6. Delivery

    A one-hour walkthrough of the final report and the agreed, ranked fix list.

    Week 3

Why accessibility is part of the audit

Fix it for some users, and it works better for all of them

Accessibility problems are usability problems with a sharper edge. The fixes that help people with disabilities also help everyone using your product in real life: reading in bright sunlight, tapping one-handed on a crowded MRT, or helping an older parent through sign-up.

Clearer text, bigger buttons and forms that explain their errors mean fewer drop-offs and fewer support calls. And it's a growing market: more than one in five Singapore citizens is already 65 or older, rising to more than one in four by 2035.

What the Accessibility Blockers Check covers

Ten focused checks against WCAG 2.2 on the same three journeys, from contrast, form labels and keyboard access to touch targets and a screen reader pass. You get a pass or fail scorecard, the critical blockers and a quick-fix guide for your developers. It's included in Complete, or S$750 as an add-on to Essential. It isn't a full conformance audit or certification, so if you need formal evidence for a government or EU contract, mention it on our call.

Why not a cheaper report or an AI tool?

Automated tools and template audits will list problems. They can't tell you which ones matter to your business, or get your team to agree on what to do.

Judgement tied to your goals

Findings are weighed against what your business is trying to achieve, drawing on 20+ years of product and programme work.

A workshop, not just a PDF

Your team leaves with priorities they agreed themselves. Larger agencies usually keep stakeholder workshops for their enterprise packages.

An honest limit. A single expert review finds many issues, but never all of them. That's why the audit is anchored in your customer feedback, and why the Complete UX Audit package adds testing with real users.

Questions

Do we need analytics or data for this?

No. The evidence comes from feedback you already have: support emails, reviews and complaints. If you do have analytics, I'll use them too.

Can you audit our website and our app?

Each audit covers one platform. If you need both, tell me on our call and I'll quote the second platform separately.

How is this different from the Discovery Sprint?

The audit fixes what already exists. The Discovery Sprint decides what to build next. If users can't work your product out, start with the audit. If they don't want it, the Sprint is the better fit.

How is this different from the AI Opportunity Assessment?

The audit looks at a live product and finds why users get stuck. The AI Opportunity Assessment uses the same method on how your business works, to find where AI could help and where it couldn't.

Do you offer accessibility checks on their own?

Not as a separate service. The Accessibility Blockers Check is included in UX Audit: Complete, or can be added to Essential for S$750. If accessibility is your main concern, for example for a government or enterprise contract, tell me on our call and we'll scope the right option.

Will you fix the issues for us?

The audit doesn't include design or build work. Most fix lists can be picked up by your own team or developers. If you'd like help with the redesign, we can scope it separately.

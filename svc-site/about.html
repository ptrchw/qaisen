Why I work this way

Running a business today means competing harder with fewer of the right people. Customers expect more with every release, and what users need keeps shifting faster than most roadmaps can follow. Meanwhile, experienced UX and product talent is scarce, every permanent hire adds overhead, and there's growing pressure to become AI-ready without a clear picture of what that should look like.

I've spent more than 20 years on the other side of that problem: founding and running a digital consultancy, leading a UX practice, building training programmes and leading experience design on national programmes. That taught me what the market expects, what quality work looks like, and how to frame problems and solutions with users at the centre.

These fixed-scope offers put that experience to work on demand. Whether you need to build your team's capability, get UX work done, shape a new product idea or see a challenge through an expert lens, there's a defined engagement to meet it, without the cost and commitment of a permanent hire. And if you're already hiring, a UX Audit or Discovery Sprint keeps your product moving until the right person joins.

Design Thinking is the framework behind all of it. Today I integrate GenAI into it, and train your team to do the same, so the work is faster and on point, meets your customers' needs and moves your business goals forward.
